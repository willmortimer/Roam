# Third-Party Notices

Roam bundles third-party fonts in the iOS app under `Roam/Roam/Resources/Fonts`.

The local `/fonts` directory is treated as an ignored source cache. The files actually shipped in the app are the curated subsets under `Roam/Roam/Resources/Fonts`, and the relevant license texts are tracked in this repository under `licenses/fonts/`.

## JetBrains Mono Nerd Fonts subset

- Bundled files live under `Roam/Roam/Resources/Fonts/JetBrainsMono/`
- These bundled files are a curated subset of patched JetBrains Mono Nerd Fonts assets
- The included license text identifies the upstream font family as JetBrains Mono and licenses it under the SIL Open Font License 1.1

- Full license text is included in `licenses/fonts/JetBrainsMono-OFL-1.1.txt`
